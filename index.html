How to print or save as PDF

  1. Open this page or any month page.
  2. Choose Sunday or Monday week start, and whether to show holidays.
  3. Click Print / Save as PDF, or use your browser’s Print command (Ctrl/Cmd+P).
  4. In the print dialog, select your printer — or choose Save as PDF / Microsoft Print to PDF.

Navigation and buttons are hidden in print layout so the calendar stays clean on Letter or A4 paper.

FAQ

Is this calendar free?

Yes. You can view, print, and save it as PDF at no charge. There are no ads or sign-up walls on this practice site.

Which holidays are included?

US federal holidays for 2027 only, using OPM observed dates. State, religious, and company holidays are not included.

Does my calendar data leave my device?

No. The calendar is generated in your browser. Printing and saving as PDF also happen locally. See the Privacy page.

Why do some holidays say “observed”?

When the legal holiday falls on a weekend, federal employees usually observe it on the adjacent Friday or Monday. OPM lists those observed dates; we mark them clearly.
