Privacy

Summary

This site is a static calendar. Calendar layouts and print/PDF actions run in your browser. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not add advertising or analytics scripts on these pages.

What stays on your device

  • Week-start and “show holidays” preferences may be stored in your browser’s localStorage so they persist between visits on the same device.
  • Printing and “Save as PDF” are handled by your browser and operating system.

What this site does not do

  • No user accounts or login
  • No form that uploads personal calendar data to a server
  • No third-party ad networks or analytics tags in the site source

Hosting and access logs

When the site is published on a host, the hosting provider may keep standard web server access logs (for example IP address, URL, and user agent). That is normal for any public website. This privacy page does not claim that the host records nothing.

External links

Links to OPM and other external sites are under those organizations’ own privacy policies.
