About this site

This is a small practice website: a free, printable English calendar for the year 2027. It was built to learn the full path from a static page to a public HTTPS site—not as a commercial product.

What you get

  • A year overview and twelve month pages
  • US federal holidays for 2027 (OPM observed dates)
  • Sunday or Monday week start
  • Browser print / Save as PDF (no app install)

Limitations

  • 2027 only — other years are not included
  • US federal holidays only — not state, local, religious, or school calendars
  • No accounts, cloud sync, or custom event editing
  • Holiday markers are for convenience; always verify with OPM for official purposes

Holiday source

Federal holiday dates follow the U.S. Office of Personnel Management 2027 schedule. Observed weekend adjustments are labeled on the pages.
